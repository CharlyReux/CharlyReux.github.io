Charly Reux

I am a PhD student in Software Engineering, working in the DiverSE team at Inria Rennes.

I work on AI for Software Engineering, more precisely on generation and verification of code-generated artefacts. I first focused on modifying code that produces visuals from high-level descriptions using LLMs and Iterative Refinement, then shifted to Reproduction of Computational Research using coding agents. Always centering my research around the evaluation of these systems through visual verification.
